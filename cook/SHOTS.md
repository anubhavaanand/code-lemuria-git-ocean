# Cook shots — Git Ocean deep-ocean theme

No reference images were attached, so shots are defined from the brief's theme:
gitcity-quality deep ocean, one seafloor, GitHub data as animals, properly structured 3D.

## S01 — Establishing deep-ocean wide shot
Camera: high angle, looking down across the seafloor basin, 45° elevation, dolly back to show scale.
Framing: wide, shows seafloor + water column + surface glow above.
What to take from gitcity: density of information, clear landmark hierarchy, everything readable at a glance.
Theme elements: deep-ocean blue/teal gradient, seabed as the single floor, distant repo-whales as silhouettes, faint god-rays from surface.

## S02 — Mid shot: a repo as a whale
Camera: eye-level with a medium whale, 3/4 rear-quarter view.
Framing: whale fills 40% of frame, seafloor visible beneath, kelp-tower repos in background.
What to take from gitcity: each entity carries readable data (name, stats) without UI text clutter.
Theme elements: procedural whale skin (mottled, wet, scarred), barnacles/pleats as data points, bioluminescent accents.

## S03 — Close-up: endpoint/animal detail
Camera: close on one animal's head or a kelp tower's top, shallow depth.
Framing: tight, shows skin/scales/coral texture and small creatures around it.
What to take from gitcity: detail density up close, nothing looks copy-pasted.
Theme elements: procedural skin texture, anemones/urchins/copepods as per-repo fauna, varied per instance.

## S04 — Seafloor / one-floor read
Camera: low, near the seafloor, looking slightly up at the water column.
Framing: seafloor fills lower third, water column above, surface light filtering down.
What to take from gitcity: clear ground plane that anchors everything, one floor not multiple levels.
Theme elements: sediment, rocks, metro tubes buried in seafloor, obelisk landmark.

Light/time of day for all shots: deep-ocean "noon" — surface light filtering from above, bioluminescence below, no stars, no near-black background, no space look.
Motion: very slow, subtle — whale swimming on its own, camera drift minimal, kelp swaying.
